# Space Situational Awareness

## EU SST (Space Surveillance and Tracking)
**Status**: REGISTERED ONLY
**Access**: RESTRICTED

Capabilities:
- Collision risk assessment
- Space debris tracking
- Re-entry prediction
- Conjunction analysis

## ESA Space Safety
**Status**: REGISTERED ONLY
**Access**: OPEN (publicly accessible services)

Capabilities:
- Space debris
- Near-Earth objects
- Space weather
- Collision risk

## Important Notes
- NOT all EU SST data is public
- Access policy must be respected
- No unauthorized access attempts
